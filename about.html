About Lumen

Lumen Desktop — Entertainment desktop mock in the browser.

This is not macOS, Siri, Apple Intelligence, or the App Store.

Official Apple icons and wallpapers are not used. Icons are original SVGs with layered glass highlights, refined specular depth, and detailed approximations. Surfaces are original glass-style CSS inspired by Liquid Glass (Golden Gate refinements, Lumen 93).

Aura is a local helper + optional cloud AI (via pollinations.ai) that can open mock apps, save notes, add reminders, do simple math, answer questions, and speak. Voice input supported where available.

Lumen 93 adds further enhanced Liquid Glass with better refraction, dynamic highlights, improved Aura AI responses, refined original icons, smoother animations, and better dock physics — still original marks only. Changed enough to be a distinct entertainment mock.

All apps are functional mocks for entertainment. Not affiliated with Apple.
