Lumen

Entertainment desktop mock in the browser.

This is not macOS, Siri, Apple Intelligence, or the App Store.

Official Apple icons and wallpapers are not used. Icons are original SVGs with layered glass highlights. Surfaces are original glass-style CSS inspired by Liquid Glass (Golden Gate refinements).

Aura is a local helper that can open mock apps, save notes, add reminders, do simple math, and speak. Settings can enable an optional third-party text demo for longer answers.

Lumen 80 adds better widgets, refined glass, and window fixes — still original marks only.
