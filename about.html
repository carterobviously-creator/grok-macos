About Lumen

Lumen Desktop — Entertainment desktop mock in the browser.

This is not macOS, Siri, Apple Intelligence, or the App Store.

Official Apple icons and wallpapers are not used. Icons are original SVGs with layered glass highlights and refined specular depth. Surfaces are original glass-style CSS inspired by Liquid Glass (Golden Gate refinements, Lumen 92).

Aura is a local helper + optional cloud AI that can open mock apps, save notes, add reminders, do simple math, answer questions, and speak.

Lumen 92 adds enhanced Liquid Glass with better refraction, dynamic original wallpaper, improved Aura AI responses, refined icons, and smoother animations — still original marks only. Changed enough to be a distinct entertainment mock.
